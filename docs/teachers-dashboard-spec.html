What exists today

The CRM has no concept of a teacher

Roles today are founder, assistant, student — that's the whole list in src/lib/staff-db.ts, and StaffGuard only knows two gates: staff (founder or assistant) and founder. There is no third door.

The pieces a teacher page would sit on top of are partly there. File upload already works (lms_resources → student-files bucket). Presence, submissions, coins and certificates all exist. What's missing is everything that makes teaching schedulable and accountable: who teaches whom, when the class is, and what happened in it.

The one thing to get right

A teacher must not be an assistant with a nicer page

Every CRM table is gated by is_crm_staff() — founder or assistant, all rows.

If a teacher is created as an assistant, they can read every student, every lead, every payment and the revenue board — and RLS will happily allow it, because the policy never asks whose student it is. The teacher role has to arrive with its own database function (is_teacher()) and row-scoped policies: a teacher sees the students assigned to them and nothing else. This is the part that has to be built first, not retrofitted.

The founder's side

What you get at /admin/teachers

The teacher page is only half of it. The reason to build this is the view it gives you: one table of every teacher with classes taught, students held, attendance rate and reports owed — so an underperforming teacher is visible in a glance rather than in a complaint.

From there you create teacher accounts (same email + password flow that creates assistants today), assign or move students between teachers, read every lesson report, and see the activity log of what each teacher uploaded and when.

Decided

The four calls, and how they landed

Phone numbers · masked

A teacher sees +2126••••11 — the masking happens in SQL, so the real number never reaches the browser. The WhatsApp button hits /api/teacher/wa/[studentId], which checks the assignment server-side and 302s to wa.me. Full numbers stay founder and assistant only.

Classes · group and private

One class_sessions table covers both, with a free-text meeting link so WhatsApp video, Meet, Zoom or an in-person room all work.

Pay · hours now, money later

Hours and class counts accrue from the first scheduled class. pay_model and hourly_rate_mad exist on the profile but only the founder can write them — a database trigger pins those fields, along with the rating, against self-editing.

Home · its own subdomain

teachers.inglizi.com, its own dashboard and its own design — warm paper, white cards, a top bar instead of the CRM's black sidebar, and the tutor-platform furniture: profile card, rating, review distribution, student feedback.
